Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

LetterHelper.ca — Free Canadian Letter Generator

1. Introduction

Welcome to LetterHelper.ca. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

LetterHelper.ca is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not:

  • Collect or store any personal information you enter into our letter generators
  • Require you to create an account or sign up
  • Store your letters, names, addresses, or any form data on our servers
  • Sell or share your personal data with third parties

All letter generation happens entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the service. Google Analytics collects anonymous data such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • General geographic location (country/city level)
  • Browser type and device type
  • How you arrived at our site (search engine, direct, etc.)

This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies, primarily from Google Analytics for anonymous usage tracking. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies for marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous website analytics
  • Google Fonts — font rendering
  • jsPDF & PDF-lib — client-side PDF generation (runs in your browser, no data sent to servers)

6. Children's Privacy

LetterHelper.ca is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website at letterhelper.ca.
