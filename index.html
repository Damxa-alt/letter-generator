Frequently Asked Questions

Are these letters accepted by IRCC and Canadian government?
Our letters follow standard Canadian formats commonly accepted by IRCC, banks, landlords, and employers. However, requirements vary by institution. We always recommend verifying with the requesting party before submitting.
Do I need to create an account?
No. All tools on LetterHelper.ca are completely free with no signup required. Your data stays in your browser and is never stored on our servers.
Can I edit the letter after generating it?
Yes. After generating your letter, you can copy the text and paste it into any word processor (Word, Google Docs) to make further edits before printing or saving.
Is the invitation letter valid for a Canadian visitor visa application?
An invitation letter is a supporting document for a visitor visa (TRV) application. It is not a guarantee of entry. The visa officer makes the final decision. Our template follows the standard format expected by IRCC.
Do I need to get the letter notarized?
Most letters generated here do not require notarization for everyday use (banking, rentals, employment verification). For legal or immigration proceedings, consult a notary or immigration consultant.
